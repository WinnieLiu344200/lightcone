# Lightcone · Sci-Fi Universe

> **v0.1 — first upload.** A high school project: an interactive 3D journey through the worlds of science fiction, running right in the browser.
> English / 中文 — switch languages with the button in the top-right corner.

You sit in the cockpit of the **Lightcone**, a one-seat ship that can open wormholes into the universes of novels. Your AI navigator, **Xiaozhou**, flies you there and explains the real science behind each story.

### The Three-Body Problem
- **Trisolaris:** fly to a planet with three suns, then land in the desert.
- **The Three-Body Game:** seven volumes, from King Wen of Zhou and Mozi to Qin Shi Huang’s army of thirty million soldiers working as a computer.
- **The Three-Body Gallery:** a museum station covering the whole trilogy, with interactive science demos. Topics include chaos, sophons, the Dark Forest, the dual-vector foil and Universe #647.

### The Wandering Earth *(in progress)*
- **Cruise:** orbit the frozen Earth and Jupiter while Xiaozhou tells the story.
- **Story game:** the ship-vs-Earth debate, a generation-ship simulation, the underground city, the magma evacuation, the rebellion and the helium flash.
- **Surface mission:** restart a stalled planetary engine during the Jupiter Crisis.

## The science inside
The three-body problem and chaos · numerical integration · gravity assists and the Roche limit · escape velocity · special relativity and time dilation · rotating space habitats and the Coriolis force · the Fermi paradox

## How to run
1. Keep `lightcone.html` and the `data` folder together.
2. Open `lightcone.html` in Chrome or Edge on a computer. A mouse and keyboard work best.
3. Click to start, and turn the sound on.

**Controls:** drag to look around · W A S D to walk · E to interact · Esc for the menu
